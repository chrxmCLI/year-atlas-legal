Privacy

Your health data stays on your iPhone.

Effective 22 October 2026 · Last updated 22 October 2026

Year Atlas is an independent iOS app made by Chris HQ. This policy explains what the app reads, where it goes, and what you can do about it. The short version: the app reads Apple Health on your device, builds your year there, and does not send your health data anywhere.

No account. No server. No tracking.
Year Atlas does not collect, transmit, sell or share personal data. There is no analytics SDK, no advertising SDK and no third-party SDK of any kind.

1. Data the app reads

With your permission, Year Atlas reads the following categories from Apple Health, for the year you choose to view:

  • Workouts (activity type, start time, duration, distance, active energy)
  • Walking and running distance, cycling distance, swimming distance and related distance types
  • Active energy burned
  • Apple exercise time
  • Flights climbed
  • Resting heart rate
  • Heart rate variability (SDNN), when present
  • Sleep analysis

If you use Garmin, these categories may be written to Apple Health by Garmin Connect. Year Atlas does not connect to Garmin directly, does not ask for your Garmin password, and does not use a Garmin API key.

2. How the data is used

The data is used only to draw your year map, calculate your year totals, show day details, and render a poster that you choose to export. All calculation happens on your iPhone. The app does not use health data for advertising, marketing, profiling, or any purpose other than showing it back to you.

3. Where the data is stored

Year Atlas keeps a local cache of the aggregated daily values in the app's Application Support folder on your iPhone. The cache is protected with iOS file protection and is excluded from iCloud backup. It is not stored in iCloud, on a server, or in any third-party service.

4. What is not collected

  • No name, email address, phone number or account identifier
  • No precise location or GPS tracks
  • No contacts, photos, microphone, camera or advertising identifier
  • No usage analytics, crash analytics or session recordings
  • No health data sent off the device

5. Apple Health permissions

Apple Health access is read-only. Year Atlas never writes to Apple Health. You can review or revoke access at any time in the Health app under Sharing → Apps → Year Atlas, or in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Health. Revoking access stops future reads; it does not delete the local cache. You can clear the cache in Year Atlas under Settings → Clear cached year.

6. Purchases

The optional Year Atlas Pro unlock is a one-time in-app purchase processed by Apple. Apple handles payment and provides the app with a transaction receipt. Year Atlas does not receive your payment details. Apple's privacy policy applies to the purchase. The app stores only a local flag indicating whether the unlock is active, and checks Apple's StoreKit entitlement.

7. Children

Year Atlas is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from anyone. Because the app collects no personal data, it does not create profiles of any user, including children.

8. Data deletion

Delete the app to remove the local cache. You can also use Settings → Clear cached year inside the app. Because no data is stored on a server, there is no server-side data to delete and no deletion request to file.

9. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date. Material changes will be reflected in the app's App Store release notes.

10. Contact

Questions about privacy? Open an issue at github.com/chrxmCLI/year-atlas-legal/issues.

11. Not medical advice

Year Atlas is a visualization tool, not a medical device. It does not diagnose, treat, cure or prevent any condition. Do not use it to make medical decisions. Talk to a qualified clinician about your health.
